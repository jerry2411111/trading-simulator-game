Trading Simulator Game
======================

1. Double-click "Trading Simulator Game.exe".
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is new and not code-signed:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".

2. The first screen asks for a free Alpaca paper-trading key (about 3 minutes, no bank account or ID):
   sign up at https://alpaca.markets, open the Paper Trading dashboard, generate an API key,
   and paste the Key ID and Secret Key into the game.

3. Trade! Your key, saves and downloaded market data stay in the "data" folder next to the .exe.
   Nothing is sent anywhere except to Alpaca to download market data.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge WebView2 (already installed on almost every PC).

Source code and full guide: https://github.com/jerry2411111/trading-simulator-game
Educational only, not financial advice.
